## Not verified, and known gaps

- **Image and CI:**
  - The CI workflow has never run on GitHub.
  - A local `docker build` is killed while Vite builds, because the Docker VM
    here has 1.4 GB.
  - The runtime layout was verified outside Docker instead: the bundle starts
    from the deployed production `node_modules`.
- **Transports:** email and Teams are not built. The outbox sends through the
  log transport only.
- **Real-world checks:** none yet; that is the A+ evidence.
  - real phones and real dictation;
  - screen readers and 200% zoom;
  - a pilot;
  - compliance and HR sign-off.
- **Tests not written:**
  - concurrent signatures on real Postgres (I14);
  - a designated observer signing end to end;
  - the conversation auto-miss sweep (it was exercised once by hand, but no
    permanent test exists).
- **The Postgres lane runs files one at a time**, because parallel files
  stalled on a small Postgres instance. The cause was not found.
