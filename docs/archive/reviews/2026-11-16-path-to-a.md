# Path to A: the plan for the next build round

Guidance from the independent reviewer, asked for by the owner after
the [first review](./2026-11-16-independent-review.md). The owner's goal:
**this becomes the hub that coaches and advisors open almost daily, and
certainly weekly.**

The numeric thresholds below are the reviewer's proposals. The owner confirms
or changes them before re-review, because the re-review grades against what is
written here.

**The rule everywhere: an A is provable from the repository. An A+ needs
evidence from real people or real operations.** No A+ is awarded on seed data.

## 2. The hub

**Set the target honestly.** Coaches open it daily, because it is their
working desk. Leaders and advisors open it weekly. Chasing daily use from
advisors would need the engagement mechanics the principles forbid.

Measure "active" by meaningful acts: signed, logged, acknowledged, held a
conversation, closed a commitment. Never page views, and never show anyone
their own activity count.

**Pull by notification, land on one action.** Every transition that makes
something wait on a person writes a row to a transactional outbox, in the same
transaction as its audit event. A dispatcher in the existing scheduler sends
it by email or to the Teams activity feed, with a deep link to one action.

**Content policy (ADR 0018, invariant I20).** A message carries only the
recipient's first name, a colleague's display name, a verb from a closed
template set, counts, dates and a link. Never narrative, reflection, levels,
hold reasons or `client_ref`. The notification row has no free-text column;
templates take typed parameters. Sign-in must return the person to the deep
link. No Teams tab yet (`frame-ancestors 'none'` stays until its own ADR).

**Immediate notifications, five only:** a request to observe; an observation
about you signed; an observation returned to you; a plan or engagement
proposed to you; one reminder 48 hours before a request expires. Everything
else waits for the digest. People choose the channel or turn it off.

**Weekly digest,** Monday 07:30 in the tenant's time zone, skipped when there
is nothing to say. Numbers and dates only, one link per line, no comparisons,
never "behind".

**Home becomes "Today".** Keep the single merged queue and add two bands:

| Band               | Coach                                                              | Leader                                           | Advisor                                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Today              | Conversations today; planned work naming you as observer           | The same, plus 1:1s                              | Planned activities due; conversation today                        |
| Needs you (exists) | Requests, drafts, proposals                                        | Requests, plans to review                        | Returned items, unrequested interactions, holds                   |
| This week          | Up to three people to observe, each with a reason; commitments due | Up to two people to observe; a coverage sentence | Next-step benchmark; open commitments; who agreed to observe what |

"Today" needs no calendar: the plan already records due dates, intended
observers and modes.

**Who to observe this week** is a pure, table-tested domain function. Priority:
an open request closing soonest; planned work this week naming you; longest
since anyone observed a focus skill; "you would be their second observer".
Capacity is self-set, default two a week. It never ranks people by progress.

**Quick capture.** One screen: who (chips, most likely first), when (today),
mode ("I was there"), skills (the person's focus skills), then level with its
descriptor inline, and a comment using the phone's own dictation. "Save and
finish later" keeps an observer-only draft that still expires. Installable as a
web app. Bars: 60 seconds from tap to signed for an observer, 30 seconds to log
for an advisor, both measured.

**Conversations and commitments are the weekly rhythm.** A conversation
(scheduled, held, missed, cancelled) hangs off an engagement or a reporting
line, so a leader's 1:1 is the same object. Its agenda assembles itself. A
commitment comes from an observer's "next step" or from a conversation,
carries forward until done or dropped, and never earns credit. Private coach
notes wait for compliance to answer the retention question.

**Recognition** is a named person's words, delivered promptly: the subject is
notified at signing (the quote only in the app), acknowledge is one tap, and
the observer sees "Maya read this". No public feed, reactions or counts.

**Program-owner adoption view.** A new `program_owner` grant. Aggregates only,
minimum cell size five, no names: time-to-signature by week, coverage, expiry
rate, distinct observers per person, acknowledgement and review rates,
first-hand share, feedback-only share, observer load (a histogram, never a
ranking), notification-to-action conversion. No individual page tracking.

## 3. Sequencing

Contract first. The schema lands once, then the API stubs, then everything
forks.

- **Wave 0 (backend owner):** one migration with every one-way door:
  `job_family` and `person_position`; `skill_profile` with `profile_key` pins;
  `observer_designation` and the `designated` basis; `notification` and
  `notification_preference`; `coaching_conversation` and `commitment`; the
  `program_owner` grant; `retention.ts`. Then contract stubs in
  `packages/api`.
- **Wave 1, in parallel on disjoint paths:**
  - Backend, in this order: outbox and digest; brief bands and the suggestion
    function; designated observers; adoption and the 12-week seed;
    conversations and commitments; CSV import, deprovisioning, duty separation,
    catalog management.
  - Web: overflow and inline descriptors (now); one-screen capture and manifest
    (now); the new Home; conversations, designation, adoption and keeper
    screens.
  - Tests and CI: Postgres lane, docs check, fake-OIDC auth tests, tenant-leak
    sweep, architecture tests, `lint:strict` (now); journeys, capture timing
    and axe as packages land.
  - Docs: ADRs 0018–0021 and a proposed calendar ADR, glossary, banned terms,
    runbooks, controls; then QA reports and the evidence manifest.

**Not yet:** calendar ingestion, the Teams tab, expectations by job family,
level 4 badges, peer witness notes, programs and cohorts, AI extensions
(including AI-written digests), a native app, a queue or worker service,
per-person analytics for program owners.

## 4. How each mark is verified

The team produces `docs/reviews/evidence/<date>.md`: each command, its output
and the commit. At re-review the reviewer reruns them and tries to break five
invariants by hand.

| Area    | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product | Capture time from a Playwright spec at 390 px, median of ten runs. A seeded adoption view showing the five vision metrics. An outbox row for each of the five triggers.                                                                                                                 |
| Domain  | I15–I20 in `invariants.md`, each with an existing test title. Interview notes filed. Open questions dated.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Roles   | The role-by-action matrix test. Journeys `directory-import-exceptions` and `deprovision-leaver`.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Data    | Retention test, table-writer test, upgrade test, integrity tests per new table.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Code    | Journeys `designated-observer-signs`, `void-reverses-and-revokes-badge`, `review-upheld-by-compliance-only`, `advisory-never-clears`, `notification-carries-no-content`, `conversation-carries-commitments`, `session-expiry-and-signout`, `two-tenant-leak-sweep`. Zero lint warnings. |
| UX      | Overflow assertion green for every persona and route at four widths. Axe report. QA reports for sign, capture, brief, conversation and review.                                                                                                                                          |
| Tech    | A `test:pg` CI job. The auth test file. The image smoke step. The Node version guard.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Docs    | `docs:check` green; no dead paths; banned terms pass; the ADR index matches the directory.                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## 5. Where chasing the letter would be wrong

- **Product and UX A+ need real users.** Do not simulate a pilot with seed
  data, and do not add celebratory motion. An honest A with a pilot plan is the
  right outcome this round.
- **Roles stop at A- until IT confirms the Entra `manager` attribute is
  reliable.** CSV import with an exceptions queue is right for now.
- **Skill profiles get columns, not behavior.** Expectations by job family
  edge toward performance management. Wait for HR.
- **The stack stays as it is.** A queue, cache or worker service would
  contradict ADR 0011 at this scale.
- **Eight strong journeys, not eighty.** Mirrors and coverage padding are
  marked down.
- **Docs: fewer and true.** An A comes from the docs check and from deleting
  stale claims, not new pages.
- **AI calibration stays at B+ until infosec clears the provider** (open
  question 15).
- **Daily use by advisors is the wrong goal.** Weekly is right. Do not put a
  daily-active number for advisors on any screen.
