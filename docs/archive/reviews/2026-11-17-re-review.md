## Invariant attacks

Every invariant attacked through the API held: I15–I17, I16/I1/I4/I32,
I21 (11 API and 5 SQL attempts), I20 (prose in every outbox column), I26,
I27, I31 and I30.

- **I2:** one database-level break. A credit inserted directly against a
  drafting observation, at a level the rating did not have, was accepted.
- **I32:** one break in the brief. Conversations late in the tenant's day were
  missing from Today, because the day was cut at UTC midnight.
- **I14:** concurrent signing showed no anomaly in one trial, which is not
  proof.

## Is it a hub?

- **Coaches, daily: plausible.** Home is a working desk.
- **Leaders, weekly: plausible** once the digest reaches them.
- **Advisors, weekly: weakest.** Their pull is "someone signed something about
  you" and the digest, and neither reaches a person yet.

The single change that would most increase weekly use is **one real delivery
channel end to end**, with truthful messages.
