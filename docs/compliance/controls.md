# Controls matrix

A SOC 2 report attests to the **organization's** controls over a period. This
application supplies the _technical_ controls and the _evidence_ auditors
sample. Some controls are organizational (HR onboarding, vendor management,
incident response staffing); those rows are marked **Org**, and the
application's part in them is stated.

The matrix also maps the financial-services and AI-governance obligations
the firm's reviewers will raise: FINRA 3110 and 4511, SEC 17a-4, Reg S-P and
GLBA, the NIST AI RMF, and ISO/IEC 42001.

Status values:

- ✅ implemented, with evidence in the repository;
- 🟡 designed but not yet built;
- **Org**: owned by the organization; the application contributes evidence.

Keep each row's evidence pointing at a test, file or query that exists.

## Financial-services obligations

| Obligation                                 | How the app addresses it                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Status                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| FINRA 3110 (supervision evidenced)         | Observations attest to _behavior_. They are not supervisory reviews of client activity, and the product copy says so; compliance should confirm. Every signature is attributable (who, when, on what basis) and immutable. | 🟡 compliance sign-off                                   |
| FINRA 4511 / SEA 17a-4 (books and records) | Records are append-only and corrected by new entries. Retention defaults to at least 6 years for attestations and audit events. Whether WORM storage is needed depends on compliance's classification of observations.     | 🟡 classification                                        |
| Reg S-P / GLBA (customer information)      | Customer information is designed out. Held evidence is visible only to its author, tenant admins and compliance reviewers. Notifications carry no content (ADR 0018). Subprocessor oversight is per CC9.2.                 | ✅ design                                                |
| Reg BI / non-cash compensation             | Credit and badges never derive from production metrics and never link to pay. Points are the weight of a signed observation only: never redeemable, never totalled across people. No contests or leaderboards.             | ✅ design, ADR 0007; 🟡 written policy (open question 2) |
