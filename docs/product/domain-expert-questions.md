# Domain-expert interview guide

`open-questions.md` lists decisions for the **product owner**. This file holds
the questions only **practitioners** can answer: coaching leads, branch
leaders, advisors, compliance, HR/L&D, identity and AI governance. Each
question records:

- why it matters;
- what it unblocks in the product;
- our **current assumption**, so the conversation can confirm or correct it
  quickly.

Record answers inline: who answered, when, and the answer. Then update the
domain model or ADR it unblocks.

**Fixed with the client:** the coach, coachee and leader triangle; skills as
the unit of development; the term "development plan".

---

## A. Coaching leads and professional coaches

| #   | Question                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Why it matters / unblocks                                                              | Current assumption                                                                  |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A1  | Walk us through the last three coaching interactions you had. Where were you, what did you see, and what did you write down afterwards?                                                                    | Grounds "observation" in reality. Decides whether `saw_it` is common or rare.          | Coaches rarely sit in client meetings; most input is a debrief or a review of work. |
| A2  | How often could you realistically confirm an observation per coachee per month?                                                                                                                            | Observer capacity limits the whole product and sets the badge criteria.                | 2–4 per coachee per month.                                                          |
| A3  | When you watch an advisor, do you think in terms of behaviors (indicators), an overall level, or a story? Which would you write in 60 seconds?                                                             | Decides whether indicators stay optional (micro-CEX) and how the sign form is ordered. | Level plus one specific comment first; indicators optional.                         |
| A4  | How do you run a coaching conversation today? Do you agree commitments, and do you follow up on them?                                                                                                      | Decides whether _coaching conversation_ and _commitment_ become first-class entities.  | Not yet: built, then deferred until pilot feedback (ADR 0019).                      |
| A5  | Who else is in the room when advisors do their best work (senior advisors, specialists)? Should they be able to vouch for what they saw?                                                                   | Unblocks the `designated` observer basis and witness corroboration.                    | A leader or coach designates a colleague for up to 90 days, audited (ADR 0020).     |
| A6  | What separates "doing with support" from "doing independently" in your own words, for two skills you coach most?                                                                                           | Calibrates the level descriptors and indicators in the starter catalog.                | The seed catalog's wording. Needs your edit.                                        |
| A7  | Would you sign "not yet" (feedback without credit)? How often?                                                                                                                                             | Validates `credit: false`. Without it, observers inflate.                              | Yes, often for early-career advisors.                                               |
| A8  | How would you want to calibrate with other coaches: blind second reads, joint observations, or periodic review?                                                                                            | Shapes observer calibration (second reads are not built).                              | Occasional blind second reads, chosen at random or where readers disagree.          |
| A9  | Before a session, what do you look at first? Would an agenda of observations since last time, interactions not yet observed, commitments due, and the next step toward a badge be enough? What is missing? | The agenda of a conversation, if ADR 0019 returns.                                     | Those four items. Deferred with conversations.                                      |
| A10 | Do you keep private notes on coachees today? Where, for how long, and who else reads them?                                                                                                                 | Whether conversations get private notes, and their retention (open question 23).       | Yes, outside any system. Not in the app until compliance decides.                   |
| A11 | How many people could you observe in a week? Would a short "who to observe this week" list, each with a reason, help or feel like being managed?                                                           | The "Worth observing this week" list on Home.                                          | At most three suggestions, each with a reason. Never a ranking.                     |

## B. Branch leaders (leaders of advisors)

| #   | Question                                                                                                                      | Why it matters / unblocks                                                  | Current assumption                                                                             |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| B1  | How many direct reports do you have, and how much time per week goes to developing them versus reviewing numbers?             | Capacity, and whether leaders can be required second observers.            | 8–15 reports; under an hour a week of observation.                                             |
| B2  | Should you be able to block a plan your report agreed with their coach, or only endorse it?                                   | Plan lifecycle (`ENDORSE` is non-blocking today).                          | Endorse, never block. Endorsement is a capacity commitment.                                    |
| B3  | What would you want to see about your team, and what would feel like surveillance?                                            | Team view content and visibility of narratives.                            | Coverage and momentum only. No totals, no ranks. Narratives visible only to the direct leader. |
| B4  | Do you also coach? Who manages your coaches?                                                                                  | Resolves the "coaches never report to coaches" conflict (open question 6). | People can be both; violations are flagged, not blocked.                                       |
| B5  | If a skip-level leader wants to observe someone, should that count?                                                           | Eligibility currently allows only the direct leader.                       | No credit, unless designated.                                                                  |
| B6  | Whom would you designate to observe your reports (a senior advisor on joint meetings, a specialist), and for how long?        | Real use of designations, and whether 90 days is the right cap (ADR 0020). | Senior advisors on joint client meetings, for about a quarter.                                 |
| B7  | How often do you hold a 1:1 with each report? Would you record it here as a conversation if the agenda saved you preparation? | Whether conversations come back (ADR 0019, deferred).                      | Fortnightly; yes, if it saves preparation.                                                     |

## D. Compliance and legal

| #   | Question                                                                                                                                             | Why it matters / unblocks                                                              | Current assumption                                                                        |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Are signed observations **books and records** under FINRA 4511 / SEA 17a-4? Are they supervisory records under 3110?                                 | Retention, WORM storage, purge rules. **Blocking for production.**                     | Development records, not supervision. Keep 6 or more years. Narratives purgeable sooner.  |
| D2  | What retention applies to reflections, narratives, notes, AI checks and audit events?                                                                | `retention.ts`, purge jobs, legal holds (tracked in tickets today).                    | Attestation facts and audit kept 6 or more years; free text kept shorter.                 |
| D3  | If a reflection describes a client complaint or a specific recommendation, what must happen?                                                         | The `escalated` evidence state and who resolves it.                                    | Escalate to the compliance reviewer; never edit it away.                                  |
| D4  | May reflection text be sent to Google (Decisions API) under a DPA, paid tier, US residency? May it go to LangSmith?                                  | Live AI mode (ADR 0008); LangSmith only if ADR 0016 returns. **Blocking for live AI.** | Text only, no client identifiers, paid tier. LangSmith gets digests only unless approved. |
| D5  | Do badges or observations ever create Reg BI or non-cash-compensation concerns?                                                                      | Confirms the "development only" policy.                                                | No production metrics, no rewards, no link to pay.                                        |
| D6  | Is recording client meetings for coaching review permitted, and with what consent?                                                                   | The `reviewed_work` mode for recordings.                                               | Only where the firm already records with consent; no audio stored in the app.             |
| D7  | Is an email that says only who asked whom to observe, a date and a link acceptable, given it may be forwarded? Does it need a classification footer? | The notification content policy (ADR 0018, invariant I20).                             | Acceptable; no content beyond names, a verb, counts, dates and a link.                    |
| D8  | May a program owner see adoption figures by business function, if no figure covers fewer than five people? Is five the right minimum?                | The adoption view's suppression rule (ADR 0021).                                       | Yes, with a minimum of five.                                                              |
